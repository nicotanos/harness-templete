# Gap report — <change name>

Stage 0 (intake). Written **before** `openspec new change`. Records what the
codebase already covers and what is missing, so scope is a finding rather than
an assumption.

## Known

What already exists and is relevant — modules, endpoints, tables, prior ADRs.
Cite paths.

## Missing

What the change needs that does not exist yet. Be specific; this is the raw
material for the proposal's scope.

## Open questions

Decisions that belong to the engineer, not to you. Resolve them in
brainstorming before authoring S1 artifacts.
